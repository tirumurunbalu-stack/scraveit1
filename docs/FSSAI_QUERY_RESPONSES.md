**1. As per the e-commerce website, no products are displayed/offered for sale.**

The website now displays stores and products at https://www.scraveit.in/shop,
in three sections: Restaurants, Groceries and Dairy. Since SCRAVEIT has not
started business before the licence is issued, every store and product is a
clearly marked sample listing that shows how products will be offered and
labelled. The complete purchase flow can be followed on the site: store,
product page with price and label details, cart, checkout (address, payment
options, bill with fees and taxes) and order confirmation with status
tracking. Until the licence is issued this runs as a clearly marked demo: no
order is placed and no payment is taken. After the licence is issued, sample
listings will be replaced by onboarded, FSSAI-licensed partners and ordering
will open.

**2. Refer to FSSAI Order dt. 18.03.2026 for necessary compliance.**

We have noted the order. SCRAVEIT operates its own platform (app and
www.scraveit.in) [and does not currently participate in ONDC as a buyer or
seller app]. The obligations it sets out are applied on our platform:
display of each seller's FSSAI licence, complete product information as per
the Labelling and Display Regulations, minimum remaining shelf life at
delivery, a grievance mechanism, and safe last-mile delivery. Details:
https://www.scraveit.in/fssai

**3. Clarify the business model and inventory storage details.**

SCRAVEIT is an inventory-less e-commerce marketplace. Customers order from
FSSAI-licensed partner restaurants, grocery stores and dairies; the seller
prepares or packs the order at its own licensed premises, and a SCRAVEIT
delivery partner delivers it directly to the customer. SCRAVEIT does not
manufacture, cook, re-pack, store or hold any food or inventory, and has no
warehouse, cold store or dark store. The registered office at 20-4-656,
Macleans Road, Nellore is an administrative office only; no food is stored or
handled there. Full description: https://www.scraveit.in/how-it-works
