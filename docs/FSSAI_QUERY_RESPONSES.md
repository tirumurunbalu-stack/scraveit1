**2. Refer to FSSAI Order dt. 18.03.2026 (ONDC model)**

Noted. SCRAVEIT runs its own marketplace (app and www.scraveit.in) and does not take part in ONDC, so we comply with both the seller-app and the buyer-app obligations. These cover seller licence display, seller agreements, a picture of the front of the pack, shelf life, free mandatory information, trained delivery, delisting, platform details, the FSSAI number on bills, calories and allergens, and grievances. Item-wise compliance is in Annexure C and at www.scraveit.in/fssai.

**3. Business model and inventory storage**

SCRAVEIT is an inventory-less e-commerce marketplace. FSSAI-licensed partner restaurants, grocery stores and dairies prepare or pack each order at their own licensed premises. SCRAVEIT delivery partners carry it straight to the customer. SCRAVEIT does not manufacture, cook, re-pack, re-label or store any food, and has no warehouse, cold store or dark store. The registered office at 20-4-656, Macleans Road, Nellore is an administrative office only. See Annexure A.

**4. FC 13, 99 and 16: products sold**

FC 16: dishes freshly prepared by licensed partner restaurants.
FC 13: sealed, manufacturer-labelled health supplements and dietetic foods sold as packed by licensed partner stores. Infant foods are not promoted (IMS Act).
FC 99: sealed retail packs of baking powder, baking soda, yeast, permitted food colours and flavour essences for home use. No industrial premixes.
SCRAVEIT never opens or re-packs these products. Sample listings are in Annexure B.
