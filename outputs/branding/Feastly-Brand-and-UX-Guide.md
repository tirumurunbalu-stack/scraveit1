# Feastly — Brand & UI/UX Direction

## Brand position

**Feastly** is a premium, warm and dependable food-delivery brand. It should feel fast enough for everyday ordering, but polished enough for high-quality restaurants.

## Logo

The logo combines a delivery bag, location-pin silhouette and a smiling steaming bowl. It communicates food, delivery and a positive customer experience in one recognisable app icon.

- Use the full-colour icon on cream or white surfaces.
- Use the charcoal version only on light backgrounds.
- Maintain clear space equal to the width of the bowl around the mark.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolour randomly or place it over busy photography.

## Core palette

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary action | Coral | `#FF5A4F` |
| Brand ink | Deep charcoal | `#201B24` |
| Reward / rating | Warm gold | `#FFB547` |
| App background | Soft cream | `#FFF8F3` |
| Surfaces | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Soft highlight | Blush | `#FFE8E3` |
| Secondary text | Slate | `#706B72` |

## Typography

- Headings: bold, rounded sans-serif; 26–30 px for primary screen titles.
- Body: clean sans-serif; 14–16 px.
- Buttons: 14–16 px, semibold, sentence case.
- Prices and order totals: bold for quick scanning.

## UI system

- 8 px spacing system; 18–22 px card radii.
- White cards on a soft cream page background.
- Coral is reserved for primary actions: add to cart, place order, accept delivery.
- Gold is reserved for ratings, rewards and positive highlights.
- Use generous whitespace and one clear primary action per screen.

## Primary customer journey

1. Customer opens Home: delivery address, search, promotion, nearby restaurants.
2. Customer selects restaurant and adds customised food items.
3. Cart shows delivery fee, offer, payment method and final total.
4. Confirmation gives estimated time and order ID.
5. Live tracking shows preparation, rider assignment and arrival progress.
6. Order completion prompts rating and easy re-ordering.

## Operational screens

- Rider: online status, delivery request, pickup/delivery steps, earnings.
- Restaurant: incoming orders, preparation status, menu availability, sales summary.
- Admin: restaurants, customers, riders, commissions, coupons, zones and reports.
