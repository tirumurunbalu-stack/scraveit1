OUR STORY

Great food has a way of bringing us closer.

SCRAVEIT is a food delivery platform operated by SCRAVEIT PRIVATE LIMITED and founded by Tirumuru Balaji.

Some people search the company as SCRAVEIT Pvt Ltd. The full registered company name is SCRAVEIT PRIVATE LIMITED, an Indian private limited company registered in Andhra Pradesh.
