Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Notice version 2026-10-23.2

This policy explains how SCRAVEIT PRIVATE LIMITED (“SCRAVEIT”, “we”) collects and uses personal data in the SCRAVEIT customer app, the SCRAVEIT Restaurant, Grocery and Dairy partner apps, the SCRAVEIT Partner (delivery) app and www.scraveit.in. We are the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”). Each app shows a short version of this notice before you create an account and asks for your consent with an unticked box. You can read that notice in English or Telugu.

What we collect and why

If you order (customers)

Name, email address, mobile numberTo create and protect your account, sign you in, send order updates and help you recover your account.
Date of birthTo check you are 13 or older and, if you are under 18, to ask a parent or guardian to approve your account (DPDP Act, s.9).
Delivery addresses and map pinTo deliver your order, show stores near you and work out the delivery fee. We use your precise location only when you tap “use my location”.
Orders, bills, payment status, tips, ratings and reviewsTo fulfil orders, issue bills, handle refunds and complaints, and show your order history.
Order chat and support messagesTo coordinate your delivery and resolve problems.
Optional: username, profile photo, friends, chats and stories (Friends & chat); gender (About you)Only if you use those features, to run them. You can switch them off or remove them at any time.
Device details: notification token, crash and performance reports, device-integrity checkTo send notifications, fix crashes and stop fraud and fake accounts.
What is in your cart, the store you picked and your past ordersTo remind you about a cart you leave (a few seconds after you leave the app, and once more the next day) and to suggest food at breakfast, lunch and dinner. Never between 10 pm and 8 am, and never to anyone under 18 (DPDP Act, s.9(3)). Turn these off in Account › Preferences.

If you sell (restaurant, grocery and dairy partners)

Owner and staff names, email addresses, mobile numbersTo create accounts, sign you in and contact you about orders and payouts.
Store name, address, map location, opening hours, menu, prices, photosTo list your store and show it to customers nearby.
FSSAI licence and certificate, PAN, GSTIN, signed partner agreementTo check the business may sell food (Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006) and to meet tax law.
Bank account and IFSC, or UPI IDTo pay you and send settlement statements.
Orders, settlements, commission, tax collected, invoicesTo run orders, pay you, and file GST and TCS returns (CGST Act, 2017, s.52).
Order chat, support messages, device detailsTo coordinate orders, ring new-order alarms, fix crashes and stop fraud.

If you deliver (delivery partners)

Name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, genderTo create your account, check you are 18 or older and contact you.
Aadhaar secure QR details, live face photo, PAN, driving licence, vehicle detailsTo confirm who you are and that you may legally ride, keep one account per person, and meet tax law. From Aadhaar we keep only your name, date of birth, gender, district, state and the last 4 digits. We never see or store your full Aadhaar number, and we delete the QR photo 7 days after your application is decided.
Live location while you are online and during deliveriesTo offer you nearby orders, show customers their delivery’s progress and calculate your pay.
Bank account and IFSC or UPI ID, earnings, incentivesTo pay you, deduct and report tax, and register you as a platform worker under the Code on Social Security, 2020.
Order chat, support messages, ratings, device detailsTo coordinate deliveries, resolve problems, ring delivery offers, fix crashes and stop fraud.

Why we are allowed to use it

We use your personal data with your consent (DPDP Act, s.6), which you give with a clear tick before you create an account. Where a law requires us to keep or share data, for example tax invoices, TCS returns or food-safety records, we do so to meet that law (DPDP Act, s.7).

Who receives it

  • The store you order from: your name, the items and your order notes.
  • The delivery partner for your order: your name, phone number and delivery address, only for that delivery.
  • Customers see a store’s name, address, FSSAI number, menu and photos, and a delivery partner’s name, vehicle and live location only during their own delivery.
  • Companies that process data for us, under contract and only on our instructions:
    • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: accounts, our database (Mumbai, India), photo storage (United States), notifications and crash reports.
    • Amazon Web Services (Mumbai, India): face matching for delivery partners, including a face record kept while the account exists to prevent duplicate accounts.
    • Cloudflare: delivering photos quickly.
    • OpenStreetMap and OSRM: map tiles and routes. Only map coordinates are sent.
  • Government authorities, when the law requires it: for example GST and TCS returns, income-tax reporting, and platform-worker registration.

We never sell your personal data. Payment card details, when online payment is offered, are handled by the payment gateway and not stored by SCRAVEIT.

Where it is stored

Our database and servers are in Mumbai, India. Photos are stored with Google Cloud in the United States. The DPDP Act (s.16) allows transfers outside India except to countries the Government restricts; the United States is not restricted.

How long we keep it

Your account, addresses, preferences, chats, photosWhile your account is open. Erased within 30 days after you delete your account or withdraw consent.
Invoices, orders, settlements, tax recordsFor the period tax law sets: under the CGST Act, 2017, s.36, until 72 months after the due date of the annual return for that year (about 8 years). Your contact details are removed from these records when your account is deleted.
Seller and delivery-partner verification and payout recordsWhile the account is open, and then as long as tax and labour law requires (up to 8 years).
Aadhaar QR photo7 days after the application is decided.
Crash reportsUp to 90 days.

How we protect it

  • Everything is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and Google encrypts our stored data.
  • Each account can read only its own data. A store sees only its own orders, and a delivery partner sees only the orders assigned to them. Our database rules enforce this on the server, not just in the app.
  • Our verification team opens identity documents only through short-lived, logged links.
  • Phone numbers typed into the in-app chat are hidden automatically.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act (s.11 to s.14) you can:

  • Access: ask for a summary of your personal data, what we do with it and who we shared it with.
  • Correct and erase: correct, complete or update your data, or ask us to erase it. You can edit most details yourself in the app.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: in the app go to Account › Privacy and terms › Withdraw consent and delete my account, or use our account deletion page. We then stop using your data and delete your account. Anything done before you withdraw stays lawful.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself. Email us the person’s name and contact details.
  • Raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer. We reply within 30 days.

To use any right, email balaji@scraveit.in from the email address on your account. We may ask you to confirm it is you.

Children

You must be 13 or older to use SCRAVEIT. If you are under 18, your parent or guardian must approve your account before you can use it (DPDP Act, s.9). We never show under-18s targeted offers or track their behaviour for advertising. Delivery partners and sellers must be 18 or older.

If there is a data breach

If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you without delay. We will say what happened, what it means for you and what you can do. We will also report it to the Data Protection Board of India, as the DPDP Rules, 2025 require.

Complaints

Contact our Grievance Officer first. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India (DPDP Act, s.13(3)).

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy in a way that affects you, we update the date and version above. The apps then show you the new notice and ask for your consent again before you continue.
