Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone number.
  • Delivery addresses and the map pin you set.
  • Orders, payments status and support messages.
  • For delivery partners and sellers: the details needed for verification and payouts.

How we use it

To take and deliver your orders, process payments and refunds, provide support, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. We share only what is needed: the seller sees your order, and the delivery partner sees your name, phone and address for that delivery.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal data. Payment card details are handled by the payment gateway, not stored by SCRAVEIT.

Your choices

You can view and edit your details in your account, and ask us to delete your account by writing to balaji@scraveit.in. Some order records are kept as required by tax and food safety law.

Contact

Questions about privacy: balaji@scraveit.in, or the grievance officer.
